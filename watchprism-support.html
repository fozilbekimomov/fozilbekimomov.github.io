Watchprism Support

For Watchprism on iPhone and Android · Updated November 13, 2026

Watchprism helps you find movies and TV shows and where to stream them. You can browse and search movies, shows and people, watch trailers, check where a title is streaming, follow shows to get new-episode alerts, set release reminders and see your viewing stats. Movie and TV data comes from TMDB, and streaming availability comes from JustWatch through TMDB.

Watchprism was previously called The Movie DB. On Google Play it is listed as "Movie Database: Movies & TV" until that listing is renamed Watchprism. It is the same app.

Account and TMDB sign-in

Do I need an account?

No. Tap Skip for now on the sign-in screen and you can browse, search, see where to watch, follow shows, set reminders and buy Premium. Signing in with a free TMDB (The Movie Database) account is optional. You need it for:

  • favourites, watchlist and ratings;
  • custom lists;
  • Taste DNA, which is built from your TMDB ratings.

Watchprism has no accounts of its own. If you try to add a favourite, add to your watchlist, rate a title or use lists without signing in, the app explains that these are saved to your TMDB account and offers Sign in with TMDB.

How do I sign in with TMDB?

  1. If you don't have a TMDB account, create one in your browser at themoviedb.org/signup. Android You can also tap "Register on TMDB" on the app's sign-in screen.
  2. In the app, open the Profile tab (the person icon) → Settings, and tap Sign In.
  3. On the "Sign in with your TMDB account" screen, enter your TMDB username and TMDB password, then tap Sign In.

Your username and password are sent directly to TMDB over an encrypted connection to start a session. Watchprism does not store your password, and we never receive it.

I can't sign in

  • Use your TMDB username and password, exactly as on themoviedb.org.
  • If you forgot your password, reset it on themoviedb.org.
  • If TMDB ends your session, the app asks you to sign in again.
  • Still stuck? Email us with the error message you see.

How do I sign out?

Open Settings and tap Log out. This ends your TMDB session. Your favourites, watchlist, ratings and lists stay safe in your TMDB account.

How do I delete my account?

Watchprism does not create accounts, so there is no Watchprism account to delete. Your TMDB account belongs to TMDB, and only TMDB can delete it:

  1. Sign in on themoviedb.org in a browser.
  2. Open your account settings.
  3. Click Delete Account (TMDB account FAQ).

Deleting your TMDB account also deletes the favourites, watchlist, ratings and lists stored in it. We cannot delete TMDB accounts for you. To remove the TMDB session from your phone, use Settings → Log out. For data that the app itself keeps, see Your data and privacy.

Premium and purchases

What does Premium include?

  • No ads
  • Premium themes
  • Taste DNA: your taste profile
  • For You: personalised picks
  • What to Watch: swipe to decide
  • Release Calendar
  • Year in Movies recap
  • Precision Discover: advanced filters without the daily limit
  • Unlimited show follows with new-episode alerts (3 shows are free)
  • Saved filters
  • The "Only on my services" filter
  • Premium member status
  • Home screen widgets (Android only)

Premium does not include watching movies. To find where a title streams, open it and see Where to Watch.

Which plans are there?

  • Monthly and Annual: auto-renewing subscriptions.
  • Lifetime: a one-time purchase. Nothing renews.

The price in your currency is shown on the Premium screen before you buy. If a plan comes with a free trial and you are eligible, the Premium screen says so and shows the date you will be charged unless you cancel before then. Payments are handled by the App Store (iPhone) or Google Play (Android).

My purchase says "Purchase pending approval"

This happens when a purchase needs approval, for example through Apple's Ask to Buy, or when a payment is still processing. You can close the Premium screen. Premium turns on by itself once the purchase is approved.

Does Premium carry over between iPhone and Android?

No. A purchase made in the App Store works on devices signed in to the same Apple Account. A purchase made in Google Play works on Android devices signed in to the same Google account. Premium is not linked to your TMDB account.

Restore purchases

The app checks your Premium status with the store each time you open it, so Premium usually comes back on its own after you reinstall or switch phones. If it doesn't:

iPhone Restore on iOS

  1. Make sure you are signed in to the App Store with the Apple Account that bought Premium.
  2. Open the Profile tab → Go Premium, or go to Settings → Subscription → Go Premium.
  3. Tap Restore Purchase, below the purchase terms. iOS may ask you to sign in to your Apple Account.

Android Restore on Android

  1. Make sure the Play Store is using the Google account that bought Premium.
  2. Open the Profile tab → Go Premium, or go to Settings → Subscription → Go Premium.
  3. Tap Restore Purchase.

If you see "No purchases found to restore", check that you are using the account that made the purchase. If you see "Couldn't check your purchases", check your internet connection and try again.

Manage or cancel a subscription

Subscriptions are billed and managed by Apple or Google, and only you can cancel them. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. The Lifetime plan is a one-time purchase and has nothing to cancel.

iPhone Cancel on iOS

  • In the app: Settings → Manage subscription. This opens your App Store subscriptions.
  • Or, on your iPhone: open the Settings app → your name → Subscriptions → Watchprism → Cancel Subscription.
  • Direct link: apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions
  • Apple help: Cancel a subscription from Apple

Android Cancel on Android

  • In the app: Settings → Manage subscription. This opens Google Play, where you can change or cancel your plan.
  • Or, in Google Play: Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  • Direct link: play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions
  • Google help: Cancel, pause, or change a subscription on Google Play

Refunds

  • iPhone Apple handles refunds for App Store purchases. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com (Apple help).
  • Android See Google Play refund policies, or email us with your order number.

Ads

Why do I see ads? How do I remove them?

Ads keep the free version free. Premium removes all ads. In the free version, you can also watch a short rewarded ad to use the app ad-free for 20 minutes.

Can I stop personalised ads?

  • iPhone When the app asks to track your activity, tap "Ask App Not to Track". To change your answer later, go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking and turn tracking off for Watchprism. Ads will then not be personalised.
  • Android Open your phone's Settings → Privacy → Ads and choose Delete advertising ID or Reset advertising ID (Google help; more ad privacy settings: Google help).
  • In the EEA, the UK, some US states and other places where it applies: in the app, go to Settings → About → Privacy options to change your ad choices. This item appears when it applies to you.

Notifications

How do I turn notifications on or off?

  • In the app, go to Settings → Notifications. Here you can turn New trailers and Trending picks on or off, and send yourself a test notification.
  • iPhone Settings app → Notifications → Watchprism.
  • Android App info → Notifications. Older Android versions of the app are called "The Movie DB" on your phone.

Alerts for shows I follow and release reminders

Tap Follow on a TV show to get new-episode alerts, or set a reminder on an upcoming movie. These alerts are scheduled on your phone, so notifications must be allowed for Watchprism. You can manage them in Profile → Following and Profile → Reminders. Free users can follow up to 3 shows.

I'm not getting notifications

  1. Check that notifications are allowed for Watchprism in your phone's settings.
  2. In the app, go to Settings → Notifications → Send test notification.
  3. If the test doesn't arrive, email us with your phone model and OS version.

Reviews from TMDB users

Reviews on a title's Read All Reviews page are written by TMDB users and come from TMDB. We do not edit them. Images embedded in review text are never loaded in the app; they show as links you can open in your browser. The reviewer's profile picture, if TMDB has one, is shown next to the review.

How do I report a review or stop seeing an author?

  • Tap the ··· button on the review. Choose Report to us to email us about it (the email already names the review), or Open on themoviedb.org to see it on TMDB.
  • Choose Hide reviews from … (it shows the author's name) to stop seeing that person's reviews on your phone.
  • To see hidden authors again: Settings → Hidden review authors → Show all.

Reports reach the developer directly by email.

Your data and privacy

Read the full Privacy Policy. In short:

  • Data on your phone includes history, stats, follows, reminders, My services, saved filters and settings. We do not upload these lists to our server. To provide features, the app sends TMDB the IDs of some of them (shows you follow, recently viewed movies, onboarding genres and your selected services), and some actions, such as following a show or setting a reminder, are logged to Google Analytics with the title's TMDB ID. You can clear most of it in the app (Search history, Recently viewed, My Stats → Clear History, My services, saved filters, unfollow, cancel reminders, Settings → Hidden review authors). Day-streak history, "continue watching" progress and onboarding genres have no separate clear option; deleting the app removes all of it.
  • TMDB data such as favourites, watchlist, ratings and lists lives in your TMDB account. Remove items in the app or on themoviedb.org, or delete your TMDB account there.
  • Analytics, crash reports and our install counter are not linked to your name, and the app does not show their identifiers, so we usually cannot single out your installation. Deleting the app stops collection. To ask us about this data, email fozilbekimomov@gmail.com.

Other questions

Can I watch movies in Watchprism?

No. Watchprism shows where a title is available to stream, rent or buy. Tapping a service under "Where to Watch" opens a link in your browser, where you can continue with that service.

A movie's details or streaming info are wrong

Movie, TV and people information comes from TMDB, a community-built database. You can suggest corrections on themoviedb.org. Streaming availability comes from JustWatch and can change by country. To see the right services, set your country in Profile → My services.

Which languages does the app support?

English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, Arabic, Indonesian, Italian, Chinese (Simplified), Russian, Turkish, German, Hindi, Ukrainian and Uzbek. You can change the language in Settings.

Are there home screen widgets?

Widgets are available on Android with Premium. They are not available on iPhone yet.
