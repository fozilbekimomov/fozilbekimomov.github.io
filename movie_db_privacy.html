Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how The Movie DB ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

The app has no accounts of its own. You can use it without signing in. If you choose to sign in with an existing TMDB account, you provide your TMDB username and password (sent only to TMDB) and any list names and descriptions you write (see 3.6 The Movie Database (TMDB)). However, we automatically collect certain data through the app and integrated third-party services.

1.1 Device and Technical Information

  • Device type and model
  • Operating system and version
  • Advertising ID, app set ID, and app or installation identifiers created by Firebase (Analytics, Installations, Crashlytics, Cloud Messaging), RevenueCat and our install counter. The Advertising ID and app set ID are shared with Google and Meta for advertising.
  • IP address
  • Approximate (country or city level) location, inferred from your IP address by Google Analytics, AdMob and Meta, and your store country
  • Language and region settings

1.2 Usage Data

  • App interactions (screens visited, features used)
  • Session duration
  • App performance metrics

1.3 Diagnostics Data

  • Crash logs
  • Error reports

1.4 Purchase Data (from app version 3.3.0)

  • Premium purchase and subscription records reported by Google Play (purchase tokens, product IDs and order details)
  • A random RevenueCat app user ID created by RevenueCat's SDK

This data is processed by RevenueCat and Google Analytics (purchase events). See 3.4 RevenueCat and 3.2 Firebase Analytics.

2. How We Collect Information

Data is collected automatically by the app and by third-party SDKs integrated into it.

3. Third-Party Services

3.1 Google AdMob

We use AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect:

  • Advertising ID
  • Device information
  • IP address
  • Interaction with ads

Learn more: Google Ads Policy

3.2 Firebase Analytics

Used to understand user behavior and improve the app.

Google Analytics for Firebase collects an app-instance ID, your Advertising ID, screens viewed, sessions, in-app events (for example titles opened and features used), in-app purchase events (product, price) and your Premium status, and approximate location from your IP address. Analytics is linked to Google Ads, so this data, including purchase events, may be used for advertising and ad measurement. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, advertising use follows your choice in the consent message.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy

3.3 Firebase Crashlytics

Used to monitor app stability and fix crashes.

3.4 RevenueCat (from app version 3.3.0)

From app version 3.3.0, we use RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., United States) as our data processor (service provider). It keeps a record of Premium purchases and subscriptions and provides sales and subscription statistics on our behalf. Google Play still processes every purchase. Your Premium access is decided on your device from Google Play: RevenueCat does not grant or block Premium.

  • Who you are to RevenueCat: a random app user ID that RevenueCat's SDK creates on your device. We do not give RevenueCat your name, email address, TMDB account or any other ID, and we do not add any other information about you to your RevenueCat record. The app does not send RevenueCat your Advertising ID or Android ID.
  • When you buy Premium or tap Restore Purchase: the app sends RevenueCat the purchases Google Play reports for your Google account, including purchase tokens, product IDs and order details.
  • Earlier purchases: the same purchase data is also sent once, the first time version 3.3.0 or later starts, so Premium purchases made with earlier versions are sent too.
  • Renewals and other subscription changes may be reported to RevenueCat by Google Play (server notifications).
  • When the app starts and when you return to it, also if you never buy anything, RevenueCat's SDK connects to RevenueCat. This sends the random ID, your IP address (as with any internet request), the app version, device model, operating system version, language and store country.
  • RevenueCat is not used for advertising or tracking, and it works regardless of your answer to the ads consent message.
  • Where the data is stored: RevenueCat stores this data on Amazon Web Services servers in the United States. For transfers from the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, RevenueCat's data processing terms use the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, with the UK Addendum and the Swiss adaptations.

Learn more: RevenueCat Privacy Policy

3.5 Meta Audience Network

Some ads are served by Meta Audience Network (Meta Platforms, Inc.) through AdMob mediation. Meta may collect your Advertising ID and other device identifiers, device information (model, screen, operating system, SDK versions), your IP address and how you interact with ads (impressions, clicks), and uses them for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention.

Learn more: Meta Privacy Policy

3.6 The Movie Database (TMDB)

Movie and TV information comes from the TMDB API. What you type in search is sent to TMDB to get results. Sign-in is optional: if you sign in, your TMDB username and password are sent only to TMDB over HTTPS; the app does not store your password and keeps only a TMDB session ID and account ID on your device. Ratings, favourites, watchlist and lists (including list names and descriptions) that you create while signed in are saved in your TMDB account.

Learn more: TMDB Privacy Policy

3.7 Firebase Cloud Messaging

The app gets a notification token from Firebase. If you allow notifications, the app subscribes to topics (all users, new trailers, trending, your language, and whether you have Premium) so we can send relevant notifications. You can turn notifications off in Android settings.

3.8 Firebase Remote Config and Installations

To load app settings and roll out features, the app sends Firebase a Firebase installation ID, country, language, time zone, operating system and app version.

Learn more about how Google handles data from Firebase services (3.2, 3.3, 3.7, 3.8): Google Privacy Policy

3.9 Our Install Counter

On each start the app sends our server a random installation ID created by the app, the Android build ID (not the Android ID), the app version, the platform and the time. We use it to count installs and version adoption. The server runs on Railway and stores data in Google Cloud Firestore in the United States; your IP address appears in the hosting provider's request logs.

4. How We Use Information

  • To improve app performance and user experience
  • To analyze user behavior and trends
  • To display personalized or non-personalized advertisements
  • To detect and fix technical issues
  • To keep a record of Premium purchases and subscriptions and see sales statistics (RevenueCat, from app version 3.3.0)
  • To send notifications you have allowed
  • To prevent fraud and invalid ad traffic and to keep the app secure

5. Data Sharing

We may share collected information with third-party services such as Google AdMob and Firebase. From app version 3.3.0, purchase data is also shared with RevenueCat, which acts as our data processor (service provider) and processes it only on our behalf (see 3.4).

  • Service providers that process data only on our behalf: Firebase Crashlytics, Remote Config and Cloud Messaging, RevenueCat (from app version 3.3.0), Railway and Google Cloud Firestore.
  • Third parties that receive data and may use it for their own advertising purposes: Google (AdMob, and Google Ads through the Analytics link) and Meta (Audience Network, see 3.5).
  • TMDB receives what you send it when you search or sign in (see 3.6).

We do NOT sell your personal data.

6. Legal Basis (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), we process your data based on:

  • Legitimate interest (analytics, app improvement)
  • User consent (advertising personalization where applicable)
  • Performance of a contract (keeping a record of your Premium purchases, RevenueCat, from app version 3.3.0)
  • Legitimate interest (sales and subscription statistics and the RevenueCat connection when the app starts, RevenueCat, from app version 3.3.0)

7. Data Retention

We retain collected data only as long as necessary for analytics and app improvement purposes.

From app version 3.3.0, RevenueCat keeps purchase records while we use RevenueCat, or until they are deleted on request (see Section 10). RevenueCat does not publish a fixed retention period.

8. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures, including encryption in transit (HTTPS). From app version 3.3.0, this also applies to the app's connections to RevenueCat.

9. Children's Privacy

Our app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

10. Your Rights

  • Reset your Advertising ID
  • Disable ad personalization in device settings
  • Limit tracking via OS settings
  • Ask us to delete your RevenueCat purchase record (from app version 3.3.0): email us (see Section 12) and include your Google Play order ID to help us find it. Deleting this record does not cancel a subscription; cancel it in Google Play. If you later restore or renew a purchase, a new record may be created.
  • Ask us to delete your data (see 10.1 Deleting Your Data)

10.1 Deleting Your Data

Email fozilbekimomov@gmail.com with "Delete my data".

  • RevenueCat purchase record (from app version 3.3.0): we delete it. Include your Google Play order ID so we can find it.
  • Our install-counter record, and Google Analytics and Crashlytics data: we delete them where you can give us an identifier by which we can find your data.
  • Advertising data is held by Google (AdMob) and Meta under their own controls: reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android settings.
  • TMDB: you can delete your ratings, favourites, watchlist and lists yourself in the app or on themoviedb.org, and delete your TMDB account on themoviedb.org. Signing out ends the TMDB session and removes it from your device.
  • Uninstalling the app removes the data stored on your device.
  • Deleting data does not cancel a subscription; cancel it in Google Play.
